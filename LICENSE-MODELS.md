# Model license: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)

All 3D models in three-premade are dedicated to the public domain under
**CC0 1.0 Universal**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

That covers:

- every procedural model the library generates (the geometry, materials and
  animations returned by `createObject`), including any `.glb` you export from them;
- the HD Blender models (`hd/models/**/*.glb`, the `three-premade-hd` package);
- the preview pictures in `docs/images/`.

You can copy, modify, distribute and use the models, even commercially, without
asking permission and without attribution. Credit is appreciated but not required.

Every model also reports this itself: `object.userData.premade.license === 'CC0-1.0'`
(and `CATALOG[type].license`, `MODEL_LICENSE` from the package).
